# Better Finder privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Better Finder does not collect, transmit, sell or share any user data.

## What the extension stores

- The keywords you add, their colors and their match-case and whole-word settings, saved per site with `chrome.storage.local`.
- The popup theme you pick, saved in the extension's own local storage.

All of this stays in your browser on your device. You can remove it by clicking "Clear All" in the popup or by uninstalling the extension.

## What the extension reads

To highlight your keywords, Better Finder reads the text of the page you are viewing, inside your browser. It does this only after you open the popup on that page, or on sites where you turned on "Highlight automatically on this site". The page text is never stored or sent anywhere.

## Network access

The extension makes no network requests. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

## Permissions

- `activeTab` and `scripting`: highlight keywords on the current tab after you click the extension icon.
- `storage`: save your keywords and settings on your device.
- Optional site access: requested for one site at a time, only when you turn on auto-highlight for that site. You can turn it off again in the popup.
